Common dental emergencies we treat

Contact Hudson Valley Dental Medicine right away if you're experiencing any of the following.

  • Severe toothache or persistent dental pain
  • Broken, cracked, or chipped tooth
  • Knocked-out (avulsed) tooth
  • Lost filling or dental crown
  • Swollen gums or facial swelling
  • Tooth sensitivity caused by injury
  • Jaw pain related to dental issues
  • Any dental issue causing significant pain or discomfort
